What Are Dental Bridges?

Dental bridges are a reliable solution for replacing one or more missing teeth. A bridge consists of an artificial tooth (or teeth) anchored by crowns placed on adjacent teeth or dental implants. This treatment restores your ability to chew, speak, and smile with confidence.

Benefits of Dental Bridges

  • Restores chewing and speaking abilities
  • Prevents neighboring teeth from shifting
  • Enhances facial structure and aesthetics
  • Long-lasting and natural-looking solution

The Dental Bridge Process at Bemis Family Dental

  1. Initial Consultation: Dr. Nathaniel Bemis will assess your oral health and determine if a dental bridge is the right solution for you.
  2. Preparation: Adjacent teeth are prepared to anchor the bridge. Impressions are taken for a custom fit.
  3. Temporary Bridge: While your permanent bridge is being crafted, a temporary one will protect your teeth and gums.
  4. Placement: The permanent dental bridge is secured and adjusted for a comfortable, precise fit.

Our office uses advanced technology, such as digital impressions, to ensure a seamless process and perfect results.

Dental Bridges vs. Other Tooth Replacement Options

While dental implants are a popular choice, bridges offer a quicker, non-surgical alternative. If you’re unsure which option is best for you, our Restorative Dentistry services cover a range of treatments tailored to your needs.

Dental Bridges: common questions

What materials are dental bridges made of?

Our bridges are crafted from durable, natural-looking materials such as porcelain or ceramic.

How long does a dental bridge last?

With proper care, dental bridges can last 10-15 years or longer.

Are dental bridges covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans cover part or all of the cost of dental bridges. Contact our office for assistance in understanding your benefits.

Do dental bridges require special care?

Maintain good oral hygiene and visit us regularly for checkups to keep your bridge in excellent condition.
