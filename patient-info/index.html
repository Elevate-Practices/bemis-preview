Your first visit

What to expect at your first appointment

We take the time to understand your health and your goals before we recommend anything.

  1. Book your visit

    Call (817) 237-9701 or request an appointment online. Let us know if you're nervous or sensitive.

  2. Health history

    We review your medical history and the medications you take, since some can affect your dental care.

  3. Complete exam

    A full-mouth series of X-rays and a thorough exam of your teeth, gums, mouth, and jaw, including non-invasive 3D imaging.

  4. Your plan, explained

    Dr. Bemis walks you through what he found and your options, so there are no surprises.

Be ready

What to bring

  • A list of your medications and their dosages, including vitamins and supplements
  • Your dental insurance information, if you have a plan, so we can verify your benefits
  • Your questions and concerns. We want to hear them.

Questions

New patient FAQs

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Call us at (817) 237-9701 or request an appointment online.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with any PPO dental insurance plan. We'll verify your benefits, file your claims, and can preauthorize treatment with your carrier.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

Our in-house membership plan starts at $349 a year and includes cleanings, exams, and X-rays, plus 20% off treatment. We also offer CareCredit financing.

What happens at the first visit?

We review your medical history and medications, take a full-mouth series of X-rays, and do a comprehensive exam of your teeth, gums, mouth, and jaw, including non-invasive 3D imaging. Then Dr. Bemis talks through what he found and your options.

Do you see children?

Yes. We care for the whole family. Learn more about children's dentistry at our office.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Let us know when you book. We offer sedation dentistry and will go at your pace.
