New patients

What to expect at your first visit

Your first visit at Bemis Family Dental is about getting to know you. Bring your questions, your concerns, and a list of any medications you take.

  1. We get to know you

    We talk through your goals and review your medical history, including medications and supplements, since they can affect your care.

  2. A complete, modern exam

    A full set of X-rays and a thorough exam of your teeth, gums, mouth, and jaw, including non-invasive 3D imaging.

  3. A clear plan, explained

    Dr. Bemis walks you through what he found and your options, so you can decide what is right for you with no surprises.

Nervous about the dentist, or sensitive during cleanings? Let us know when you book. We offer sedation dentistry and will go at your pace. Everything new patients need to know.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question? Call us at (817) 237-9701 and our team will help.

Is Bemis Family Dental accepting new patients?

Yes. Bemis Family Dental welcomes new patients of all ages from Fort Worth, Lake Worth, and nearby communities. Call (817) 237-9701 or request an appointment online to get started.

What dental insurance do you accept?

We work with most PPO dental insurance plans (call us to check yours). Our team files your claims, verifies your benefits, and can preauthorize treatment with your carrier.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

You can still get care here. We offer financing through CareCredit, which lets you spread the cost of treatment into monthly payments.

Do you see children?

Yes. We see kids for checkups, cleanings, sealants, and fluoride, so the whole family can visit the same office.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us right away at (817) 237-9701. Tell us what happened and we will work to see you as soon as possible.

Does Dr. Bemis offer Invisalign?

Yes. Dr. Bemis is an Invisalign provider. Clear aligners straighten teeth without metal brackets, and you can remove them to eat and brush.

Where is your office, and what are your hours?

We are at 6717 Telephone Rd, Fort Worth, TX 76135, serving Lake Worth, White Settlement, Azle, and Saginaw. Our office is open Wednesday and Thursday, 8 AM to 5 PM.
