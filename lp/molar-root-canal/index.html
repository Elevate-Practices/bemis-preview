Is this you?

Signs your back tooth may need a root canal

Molars do most of your chewing, so when one hurts, you feel it every meal. Call us if you notice:

  • Pain that lingers, especially when you bite or chew
  • Sensitivity to hot or cold that doesn’t go away
  • Swollen or tender gums near the tooth
  • A pimple-like bump on the gum (a sign of an abscess)
  • A tooth that has darkened
  • Pain that wakes you up at night

Don’t wait for it to get worse. An infection that isn’t treated can spread and cost you the tooth. The sooner it’s treated, the better your chances of keeping it.

Questions

Molar root canal questions

How long does a molar root canal take?

Most root canals are finished in one to two visits, depending on how severe the infection is. Molars have more canals, so the visit runs a little longer than for a front tooth.

Does a root canal hurt?

We numb the area completely before we start. Most patients say it feels a lot like getting a filling. Some soreness afterward is normal and usually eases with over-the-counter pain relievers.

Will I need a crown afterward?

Usually, yes. A treated molar is more fragile, and a crown restores its strength. We can plan and place it here in our office.
