Types of Dentures We Offer

We offer two main types of dentures, tailored to your specific oral health needs:

1. Full Dentures

These are ideal for patients who are missing all teeth in the upper or lower jaw. Full dentures rest on the gums and are crafted to restore natural function and appearance. They are removable and easy to care for with proper cleaning.

2. Partial Dentures

If you still have healthy natural teeth remaining, partial dentures can fill the gaps while preserving your existing tooth structure. These appliances attach securely using discreet clasps or precision attachments for a natural look and reliable fit.

We also offer implant-supported dentures for enhanced stability—ask us if you're a candidate during your consultation.

What to Expect from the Denture Process

Receiving dentures involves a few important steps to ensure a perfect fit and long-term comfort:

  1. Initial Consultation & Impressions
    We assess your oral health, take digital impressions using advanced iTero technology, and discuss your smile goals.
  2. Fitting & Customization
    Our lab designs your dentures to match the color, shape, and size that suits your facial features and bite.
  3. Adjustments & Final Placement
    Once your dentures are ready, we ensure a secure, comfortable fit. Any necessary adjustments are made for optimal function.
  4. Ongoing Support
    You’ll receive tips for caring for your dentures and guidance on how to adapt to life with them. Regular checkups help us monitor your fit and oral health.

Full & Partial Dentures: common questions

How long do dentures last?

With proper care, dentures can last 5 to 10 years. Over time, adjustments or replacements may be needed due to natural changes in your mouth.

Are dentures uncomfortable to wear?

There is an adjustment period, but today’s dentures are designed to fit snugly and feel comfortable. We offer relining and adjustments to enhance fit as needed.

Can I eat normally with dentures?

Yes. After a short adaptation period, most patients are able to enjoy a full range of foods. Softer foods are best in the beginning.

Do partial dentures damage natural teeth?

No. Properly fitted partials are designed to protect your remaining teeth and help distribute biting force evenly across your mouth.

What if I want something more permanent?

If you’re looking for added stability, we may recommend implant-supported dentures for a secure and natural-feeling solution.
