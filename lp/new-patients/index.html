Questions

New patient questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. We welcome new patients of all ages from Fort Worth, Lake Worth and nearby communities. Book online or call (817) 237-9701.

Who qualifies for the $99 special?

New patients without dental insurance. It includes a comprehensive exam, needed X-rays and a routine cleaning, plus free take-home whitening when you complete your new patient visit. The routine cleaning applies when no gum disease is present. Not valid with insurance, government-funded plans or other offers.

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most PPO dental plans. Call us and we’ll check yours. We verify your benefits, file your claims and can preauthorize treatment.

Do you see children?

Yes. We see kids for checkups, cleanings, sealants and fluoride, so the whole family can visit the same office.

Where are you, and when are you open?

We’re at 6717 Telephone Rd, Fort Worth, TX 76135, serving Lake Worth, White Settlement, Azle and Saginaw. The office is open Monday and Tuesday 8 AM to 3 PM, and Wednesday to Friday 8 AM to 5 PM. You can book online any time.
