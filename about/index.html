Our practice

Since 2018, Bemis Family Dental has been the leader and preferred provider of quality dental services to our patients in Fort Worth and the surrounding areas. Our experienced dentists and staff offer comprehensive dental examinations and specialize in a wide array of cosmetic, preventative, general and specialized dental services.

In our mission to provide the best dental services possible, our doctors use only the most advanced, state-of-the-art technology available. We are committed to educating our patients and providing personalized dental services to the people of Fort Worth. At Bemis Family Dental, you will find dental professionals who genuinely care about your health and are dedicated to providing exceptional service to everyone who walks through our door.

Meet your dentist

Dr. Nathaniel Bemis

Dr. Nathaniel Bemis earned his undergraduate degree at Brigham Young University in 2007 and graduated from Oregon Health & Science University's dental school in 2011. He has practiced in Fort Worth and Hurst ever since.

In 2018, Dr. Dwayne Beeler chose Dr. Bemis as his successor after interviewing several dentists, and Bemis Family Dental carried on his tradition of care for Lake Worth patients. Dr. Bemis treats a wide range of needs in-house, from cosmetic and restorative work to implants, oral surgery, root canals, and Invisalign.

“Bemis Family Dental is a place where you are going to be treated by Dr. Bemis, and we are going to treat you the way that we treat our friends and family.”Dr. Nathaniel Bemis
