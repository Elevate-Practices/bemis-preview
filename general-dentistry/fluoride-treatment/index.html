What Are Fluoride Treatments?

Fluoride is a mineral that is commonly found in dental products like toothpaste and mouthwash. Its primary function is to shield and fortify your teeth, making them less susceptible to the development of cavities. At Bemis Family Dental, we offer professional fluoride treatments as an extra layer of defense for your smile. This beneficial service is suitable for patients of all ages, from children to adults.

Fluoride, a mineral commonly found in dental products like toothpaste and mouthwash, plays a crucial role in safeguarding your teeth against the perils of cavities. Our professional fluoride treatments take this protection to the next level. By fortifying your teeth with additional fluoride, we enhance their resistance to decay, ensuring a durable and resilient smile. Whether you're a child just starting your dental journey or an adult seeking to bolster your oral health, fluoride treatments are a beneficial option for all.

Why Might You Need Fluoride Treatments?

Now, you might be wondering why fluoride treatments are necessary. The reasons are as varied as our patients. For some, susceptibility to decay is a constant concern, especially among children and teenagers who may not have mastered the art of proper brushing and flossing. For others, fluoride treatments serve as a complementary approach to bolstering their teeth alongside their daily oral care routines.

Determining whether fluoride treatments are right for you is a straightforward process. During your examination at Bemis Family Dental, our expert team will assess your dental health and recommend fluoride treatments as needed. For children visiting our office, these treatments are often advised to keep their smiles vibrant and cavity-free. In reality, most of our patients, regardless of age, can benefit from the protective embrace of fluoride treatments. The frequency of treatment can be tailored to your individual needs, ensuring your smile is well-guarded.

Let's demystify the treatment process. Once comfortably seated in one of our dental chairs, we start by cleaning your teeth meticulously. Following this, a liquid fluoride solution is gently brushed onto your teeth. Post-treatment care instructions are provided, typically involving avoiding eating and drinking for about an hour to allow the fluoride to fully penetrate your teeth. The best part is that you can have these treatments as often as necessary. Many of our patients choose to incorporate fluoride treatments into their regular dental checkups to maintain the utmost protection for their smiles.

If you're eager to learn more about the benefits of fluoride treatments and how they can contribute to your oral health, reach out to our dedicated team at Bemis Family Dental today. We're here to guide you on your journey to a healthy, beautiful smile.
