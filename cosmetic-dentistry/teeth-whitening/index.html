Brighten Your Smile with Professional Teeth Whitening

Whether you're preparing for a big event, wanting to reverse coffee or tobacco stains, or simply ready to upgrade your smile, our cosmetic team, led by Dr. Nathaniel Bemis, is here to help you achieve a noticeably brighter look with long-lasting results.

What Causes Tooth Discoloration?

Tooth stains can come from many sources—some more surprising than others. Over time, teeth naturally darken or yellow due to:

  • Coffee, tea, wine, and soda consumption
  • Tobacco use (smoking or chewing)
  • Certain medications
  • Poor oral hygiene
  • Aging enamel
  • Genetics

No matter the cause, our in-office whitening can remove deep-set stains far more effectively than over-the-counter products.

How Our Fort Worth Teeth Whitening Works

At Bemis Family Dental, we use advanced, professional-grade whitening systems that deliver fast and safe results with minimal to no sensitivity.

Our Process Includes:

  1. A thorough dental exam to ensure your teeth and gums are healthy for whitening
  2. Protection of your gums and soft tissues
  3. Application of a professional-strength whitening gel
  4. Optional light-activated enhancement (depending on your case)
  5. Results up to 8 shades whiter in one appointment

We also offer take-home whitening kits for patients who prefer to whiten on their schedule, with custom-fit trays designed for comfort and uniform results.

If you’re wondering about the safety or effectiveness of whitening treatments, we encourage you to review our blog on teeth whitening safety.

Is Teeth Whitening Right for You?

While most patients are excellent candidates, a consultation helps determine if professional whitening is best for your smile goals. Whitening is not recommended for:

  • Children under 16
  • Pregnant or breastfeeding women
  • Patients with restorations on front teeth (e.g., crowns, veneers) that won’t respond to bleaching agents
  • Individuals with extremely sensitive teeth or untreated dental conditions

Not sure if whitening is your best option? Our team can recommend alternatives such as dental bonding, inlays and onlays, or a full smile makeover.

Other Cosmetic Options for a Brighter Smile

Professional whitening is a quick and dramatic solution, but some patients choose to combine it with other cosmetic dentistry treatments for even more comprehensive results. Options we offer include:

  • Invisalign clear aligners to straighten and align your smile
  • Porcelain veneers for shape, size, and color correction
  • Full smile makeovers tailored to your facial features and lifestyle

Our team uses digital impressions with iTero scanning for precise planning and beautiful results.

Teeth Whitening: common questions

How long does professional teeth whitening last?

With proper care and regular dental cleanings, results can last from 6 months up to 2 years. Lifestyle factors, like diet and tobacco use, can shorten this timeframe.

Does teeth whitening hurt?

Most patients experience little to no sensitivity. We use desensitizing agents and monitor every step to ensure your comfort.

Can I whiten my teeth if I have crowns or fillings?

Whitening agents do not affect restorations like crowns or fillings. We can recommend options to match your natural teeth post-whitening for uniform results.

Is it better to get in-office whitening or a take-home kit?

In-office whitening provides immediate results. However, custom take-home kits offer flexibility and can be used to maintain your brightness long-term.

Will whitening damage my enamel?

No. When performed under professional supervision, whitening is safe for your enamel. Avoid overuse of store-bought products or DIY treatments.
