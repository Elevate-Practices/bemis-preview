What is Sedation Dentistry?

Sedation dentistry involves the use of medication to help patients relax during dental treatments. It is especially beneficial for those with:

  • Dental anxiety or fear
  • A strong gag reflex that makes procedures difficult
  • Sensitive teeth or gums
  • A low pain threshold
  • Difficulty sitting still for long periods
  • Complex or multiple dental procedures in one visit

With sedation, you can undergo treatment with little to no discomfort while remaining fully or partially conscious throughout the procedure.

Types of Sedation Dentistry We Offer

Nitrous Oxide (Laughing Gas)

  • A mild, inhaled sedative that helps you relax without putting you to sleep.
  • Wears off quickly, allowing you to resume normal activities after your appointment.
  • Ideal for patients with mild to moderate dental anxiety.

Oral Sedation

  • A prescribed medication taken before the procedure to reduce anxiety.
  • Leaves you in a deeply relaxed state while remaining awake and responsive.
  • Effects last longer, so you will need someone to drive you home after your visit.

During your consultation, Dr. Bemis will discuss the best sedation option based on your needs and medical history.

What to Expect with Sedation Dentistry

1. Pre-Treatment Consultation

Before your procedure, we will review your medical history and determine the safest and most effective sedation method for you. We will also provide instructions on how to prepare for your appointment.

2. Relaxing During Your Procedure

Depending on the type of sedation used, you will either inhale nitrous oxide through a mask or take an oral sedative before your treatment begins. Throughout the procedure, our team will monitor you closely to ensure your comfort and safety.

3. Recovery and Aftercare

  • Nitrous oxide wears off quickly, allowing you to drive yourself home.
  • Oral sedation takes longer to wear off, so you will need a responsible adult to drive you.
  • Most patients feel relaxed and at ease after treatment, with little to no memory of the procedure.

Benefits of Sedation Dentistry

  • Reduces anxiety and fear for a stress-free dental experience.
  • Minimizes discomfort and pain sensitivity during procedures.
  • Helps patients with a strong gag reflex feel more comfortable.
  • Allows for multiple treatments in one visit, reducing the need for multiple appointments.
  • Makes dental care accessible to those who would otherwise avoid it.

Who is a Good Candidate for Sedation Dentistry?

Most patients with dental anxiety, phobia, or sensitivity are great candidates for sedation dentistry. However, certain medical conditions may require adjustments to ensure safety. During your consultation, Dr. Bemis will assess your health history and discuss the best sedation option for you.

Questions

Sedation Dentistry: common questions

Is sedation dentistry safe?

Yes. Sedation dentistry is a safe and widely used option for reducing dental anxiety. Our team closely monitors each patient throughout the procedure to ensure their safety and well-being.

Will I be asleep during my procedure?

No. With nitrous oxide and oral sedation, you will be awake but deeply relaxed. You may feel drowsy, but you will be able to respond to instructions.

Can children receive sedation dentistry?

Yes, nitrous oxide is a safe and effective option for children who experience dental anxiety.

Do I need someone to drive me home after sedation?

If you receive oral sedation, yes—you will need a trusted adult to drive you home. Nitrous oxide wears off quickly, so most patients can drive themselves.
