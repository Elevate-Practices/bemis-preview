What Makes Our Kid's Dentist Care Unique?

Dr. Nathaniel Bemis understands the unique needs of growing smiles. Our pediatric dental services are designed not just to treat, but to educate and empower children on proper oral hygiene from an early age.

Our focus includes:

  • Comfortable cleanings and checkups

Children's Dentistry: common questions

When should my child first visit the dentist?

We recommend bringing your child in by their first birthday or when their first tooth appears. Early visits help prevent future problems and establish comfort with dental care.

What if my child is afraid of the dentist?

Our team is trained in working with children of all temperaments. We explain procedures in kid-friendly language and offer a calm, supportive environment.

How often should children see the dentist?

Most children should visit the dentist every six months for cleanings and exams. We may recommend more frequent visits for certain conditions or treatment needs.

Do baby teeth really matter if they fall out?

Yes. Baby teeth play a vital role in speaking, chewing, and guiding the growth of adult teeth. Keeping them healthy is essential for long-term dental development.

Can my child get cavities even if they brush?

Yes. Brushing is important, but diet, genetics, and brushing technique all play a role. We may recommend additional protection like sealants or fluoride treatment.
